Abrakson White Water Rafting

We exist to reconnect people with the raw power and beauty of nature through unforgettable whitewater rafting adventures. Our mission is to deliver world-class whitewater rafting experiences that prioritize safety, fun, and environmental respect. We believe the river is a teacher — it builds courage, demands respect, and rewards teamwork.

History

Founded in 2012 by a group of passionate river guides and adventure enthusiasts, Abrakson White Water Rafting was born on the banks of a roaring river in Nigeria.

What started as a weekend hobby among friends who loved chasing rapids quickly grew into a mission to share the thrill of whitewater rafting with others. Today, we continue to create exciting adventures while putting safety, teamwork, and respect for nature first.
